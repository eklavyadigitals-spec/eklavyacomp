MS-CIT (Maharashtra State Certificate in Information Technology)

MS-CIT is an Information Technology (IT) literacy course initiated by MKCL in 2001. As the premier IT literacy programme in Maharashtra, it helps learners bridge the digital divide by mastering essential computer operations, digital communications, and workplace tools.

250+ Digital Skills

Covers computer hardware, Windows, smart typing, and online citizen services.

125+ AI Tools Awareness

Learn prompt engineering fundamentals and productivity tools like ChatGPT & Gemini.

MKCL ERA Learning System

Interactive self-paced e-learning modules reinforced with hands-on practice.

Recognized Certification

Jointly certified by MSBTE (Board of Technical Education) and MKCL.

Core Digital Skills Covered in MS-CIT
Computer Basics Windows Navigation Smart Typing (English & Marathi/Hindi Unicode) Microsoft Word Microsoft Excel Microsoft PowerPoint Outlook & Email Digital India & Citizen Portals Cyber Security & Safety AI Tools & Prompt Engineering Basics

How You Learn MS-CIT

MS-CIT follows an interactive, learner-centric approach combining digital media and direct computer practice:

  • Self-Paced e-Learning: Guided interactive lessons through MKCL's ERA platform.
  • Hands-on Lab Practice: Apply every lesson directly on personal computer stations.
  • Personal Support: Faculty guidance for doubt clearing and regular assessments.
  • Flexible Language Mediums: Available in Marathi, Hindi, and English.

MS-CIT (Maharashtra State Certificate in Information Technology) is an IT literacy course started by MKCL in 2001. Jointly certified by the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) and MKCL, it is recognized as a vital computer qualification across Maharashtra.

Through MS-CIT, you learn computer fundamentals, Windows OS operations, smart typing (English and Marathi/Hindi), office productivity applications (Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook), internet tools, citizen services, cyber security awareness, and an introduction to modern AI tools for everyday productivity and study.

In addition to MS-CIT, Eklavya Computers offers Tally / business accounting training and individual programming courses including C Programming, C++, Java, and Advanced Java.
