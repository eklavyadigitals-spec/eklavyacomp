Digital Literacy & Productivity

Build Essential Digital Skills with MS-CIT

MS-CIT is a comprehensive IT literacy programme designed to help learners develop practical computer and digital skills. From creating professional office documents to safe internet navigation and modern AI prompt awareness, gain the everyday tools to succeed.

Curriculum Overview

What You Will Learn in MS-CIT

A step-by-step modular curriculum based on current official MS-CIT themes, taught with 100% hands-on lab practice.

Computer & Smartphone Skills

Learn essential computer operations, file management, operating system navigation, smartphone connectivity, and digital usage.

Microsoft Word

Create and edit professional documents, reports, letters, tables, formatting styles, and print-ready files.

Microsoft Excel

Work with spreadsheets, numerical data, mathematical formulas, basic functions, data sorting, and chart visualisations.

Microsoft PowerPoint

Create effective slide presentations, animate concepts, structure visual stories, and present ideas confidently.

Digital Skills for Everyday Life

Use modern online government portals, digital payment systems, cloud storage, utility services, and search engines more confidently.

Prompt Engineering Awareness

Introduce learners to modern prompt-based interaction with digital assistants and AI tools for research and learning assistance.

Email & Productivity (Outlook)

Master formal email communication, calendar scheduling, contact organisation, and office communication etiquette.

Cyber Safety & Digital Etiquette

Understand password hygiene, safe browsing habits, phishing awareness, and safe, responsible digital behavior.

Beginners
