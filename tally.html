Detailed Syllabus

What You Will Learn in Tally

A comprehensive, industry-aligned syllabus covering core accounting fundamentals to advanced TallyPrime and GST workflows.

Fundamentals of Accounting

Understand essential accounting concepts, debit and credit rules, golden rules of accounting, double-entry bookkeeping, and transaction classification.

TallyPrime Setup & Navigation

Learn the basics of working with TallyPrime, setting up company data, security controls, company configuration, and data backup procedures.

Ledgers & Chart of Accounts

Understand and manage accounting groups, primary and secondary ledgers, account heads, sundry debtors, sundry creditors, and structured charts of accounts.

Business Transactions & Vouchers

Record standard business entries: Purchases, Sales, Receipts, Payments, Contra entries, Journal vouchers, Debit notes, and Credit notes.

Inventory & Stock Management

Learn how businesses create stock items, stock groups, units of measure, godowns/warehouses, purchase orders, sales orders, and stock summaries.

GST (Goods & Services Tax)

Understand practical GST-related transactions in TallyPrime: CGST, SGST, IGST calculations, tax invoices, GST rates setup, and return preparation overviews.

Banking & Reconciliation

Learn banking features, cheque printing, bank ledger setup, auto/manual Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS), and deposit slip generation.

Financial Reports & Analysis

Generate and analyze essential financial statements: Trial Balance, Profit & Loss Account, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Daybook, and Ledger Summaries.

Target Audience

Who Should Join the Tally Course?

Ideal for anyone aiming to develop practical bookkeeping competence for employment or business management.

Commerce Students

B.Com, BBA, and junior college students wanting practical software application of classroom accounting.

Recent Graduates

Graduates seeking job-ready skills in corporate accounting, billing, and inventory offices.

Aspiring Accountants

Individuals preparing for accounts executive, audit assistant, or billing clerk job roles.

Small Business Owners

Entrepreneurs and traders who want to manage their own bills, inventory, GST, and profit reports.

Working Professionals

Admin and office staff looking to upgrade from manual ledgers to computerized TallyPrime ERP.
